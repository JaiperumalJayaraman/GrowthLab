05 / SHOW YOUR REASONING

The product brief.

A portfolio case study should explain the problem, choice, risk, and validation plan—not just display charts.

PM STORYTELLING
01

The user

Small team founders and project leads signing up to organize work for the first time.

JOB TO BE DONE
“Help me get my team working without spending an hour setting things up.”
02

The product bet

Users facing a blank project canvas need a relevant, low-effort first step more than another feature.

MVP
Three role-based starter templates with an optional skip path.
03

The guardrails

Optimize first-task activation while monitoring setup completion, invitation rate, and longer-term retention.

SUCCESS IS NOT
More clicks, more signups, or a statistical win with worse user trust.
